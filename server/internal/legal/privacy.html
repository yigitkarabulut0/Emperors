Emperors is a game run by {{.Name}} ("we"). This policy says what the game keeps about you, why, where, for how long, and how to have it removed. It describes the game as it is today; if that changes, this page changes first.

In short: we keep what the game needs to run your account and deliver what you buy. We do not sell your data, we show no third-party advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account in the game at any time.

What we keep

  • Your account: the username and display name you choose, your password (stored only as a one-way hash, never readable, even by us), your chosen portrait, and your time zone's offset so the day resets at your own midnight.
  • Your game: your progress and everything in it: your level, gold, diamonds, gear, soldiers, kingdom, battles and their results, letters in your Royal Mail, and the history of what you earned and spent in diamonds.
  • Your purchases: when you buy something, Apple sends us a signed record of it: the product, the transaction number, the date, the price tier, the App Store country and currency, and whether it was later refunded. We never see your name at Apple, your Apple ID, or any payment card; Apple handles the payment.
  • Your phone: a scrambled, one-way form of the identifier your phone gives this game (never an advertising identifier), so that a promo code or a friend's invitation counts once per phone. The identifier itself is not kept, and the scrambled form cannot be turned back into it.
  • How the game is used: the app tells us when it is opened and closed, how long a session lasted and which screens were visited, and we note which days you played. We use this to see which parts of the game work and which do not. It contains nothing you type.

Your phone reaches our servers over the internet, so they see its IP address while it is connected, as any server does. We do not store it.

What other players see

Emperors is played with other people. Your username, display name, portrait, level, strength, kingdom and the cosmetics you wear are shown to other players: on the raid list, in the rankings, in your kingdom and in battle reports. Your purchases, your diamonds and your password are never shown to anyone.

Why we keep it

  • to run your account and keep your game the same on every device you sign in on;
  • to deliver what you buy, restore it on a new phone, and take it back if Apple refunds it;
  • to keep the game fair: to find cheating, codes redeemed twice on one phone, and refunds used to get things for free;
  • to understand how the game is played, so we can improve it.

Where the law asks for a legal basis: running your account and delivering purchases is the contract between us; keeping the game fair and understanding how it is played are our legitimate interests; keeping purchase records is a legal obligation.

Where it is kept, and who helps us

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone. It is kept on servers in Germany, with two companies that process it only on our behalf:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany), which hosts the game's servers;
  • Neon, which runs the game's database in the AWS Frankfurt region (Germany).

Apple processes your payments and sends us the purchase records described above, under Apple's own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and game: until you delete your account.
  • The record of how the game is used: 180 days; the record of which days you played: 400 days.
  • Purchase records: for as long as the law requires us to keep financial records, even after an account is deleted, so that a refund Apple makes later can still be settled.
  • After you delete your account, we keep only a small note that it existed (when it was made and deleted, its level, and its diamond balance and any refund debt), so a late refund or dispute can be settled. The history of its diamonds is removed 90 days later.

Deleting your account, and your other rights

You can delete your account in the game: open your portrait, then Delete account, and confirm with your password. Your account and game are removed at once. A subscription is billed by Apple and is not cancelled by deleting your account: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

You may also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to stop a use of it you object to. Write to {{if .Contact}}{{.Contact}}{{else}}us through the support link on the game's App Store page{{end}}. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Emperors is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep data about them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

When what the game keeps changes, for example if it one day shows adverts or adds chat, this page changes before the game does, and the date at the top moves.
